Welcome to Trattoria Trussardi!

In the heart of Morioh, where the salt air drifts in from the coast and the afternoon light stretches long across the tables, Tonio Trussardi runs a kitchen that feels more like a living room than a restaurant.

A Neapolitan by birth, Tonio came to Morioh with little more than his recipes and a stubborn belief that the right ingredients, handled with care, are enough. No frills, no pretension — just the kind of food you'd expect from a family that has been feeding people for generations. The produce arrives each morning from the local fruit parlor, the fish comes off the boats before the market closes, and the rest is left to his hands. The tables are close together, the plates are generous, and the conversation lingers well past the last bite. A puppy sleeps under the counter and gets first taste of everything. Sit down. Tell us what you're in the mood for, and we'll take it from there.

We are glad to have you here. Explore our menu and book your table for a fantastic dining experience.
